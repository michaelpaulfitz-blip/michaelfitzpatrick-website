Video Game Projects In progress

Internal dashboard for a small crew building games to play at LAN parties. First target: a faithful rebuild of Warlock, the Warcraft III custom map, with the knobs turned up to "configurable".

Introduction

Warlock is a Warcraft III custom map where every player is a wizard on a platform surrounded by lava. Spells deal damage and, more importantly, knockback: the goal is to blast everyone else into the lava while the platform shrinks. Between rounds you spend gold on new spells, upgrades, and items.

We want to rebuild it as a standalone game for our ~15-person LAN group. Success means it plays exactly like the original, plus a host settings screen (e.g. a longer shop phase so people can read the spells and loot before buying). If we nail that, we go after bonus features.

Settings wishlist

Knobs the host should be able to turn. Defaults are the original's values.

  • Shop phase length — the reason this project exists
  • Rounds per match and win condition
  • Arena shrink speed and lava damage
  • Starting gold and gold per round / kill
  • Spell and item pool (ban list)
  • Teams vs free-for-all
  • Ready-up vote to end the shop early
  • Knockback, damage, and friction multipliers

Precedent: WC3 hosts could already lengthen shop time (-c 4 / -c 5), Community Edition added a ready-up vote, and the Dota port exposed lava, knockback, friction, and gold as settings. Ready-up vote goes on the list.
