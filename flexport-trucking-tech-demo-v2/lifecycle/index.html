US Ground Freight Lifecycle

Twelve handoffs. No shared system of record.

A truck move looks linear on paper. Operationally, physical custody and the authoritative record keep changing hands , and the party controlling the decision is usually not the party paying for it.

The broker sits in the middle absorbing every mismatch: between the shipper's data, the receiver's appointment, the carrier's truck, and the facility's timestamps. Its margin is the residual after all of them.

Make the shared record the product.
