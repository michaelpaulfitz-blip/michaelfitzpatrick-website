The BPM Ladder

Fifteen kinds of electronic music, from beatless ambient up to 174 BPM drum & bass. Every card has a track you can play right on the page.

Tempo map

BPM

Bars show each genre's usual tempo range. Striped bars mean there may be no beat at all, or the tempo moves around. The ring on dubstep marks the 70 BPM it feels like, because its drums hit at half speed. Color follows tempo too, from blue (slow) to red (fast), and each card's light blinks at that genre's typical BPM.
