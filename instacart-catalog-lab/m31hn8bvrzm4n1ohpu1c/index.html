Catalog Lab: every pain point from the loop, and a working gluten-free evidence engine

Four workstreams on one page. The pain points come from all nine conversations in the loop, merged where people said the same thing. The lab collects gluten-free evidence from independent sources, scores each product with provenance, and measures precision and recall against a gold set you label. The photo reader tests whether package images can serve as a fourth source. The diagrams try several ways to draw the same systems so the arrows stay readable.

Workstream 1

Pain points from the loop

Every pain an Instacart person raised or confirmed. Multi-source pains sort first because they are the strongest signal. Each one carries real-world solutions and a few ways you could demonstrate a fix, tagged by whether they fit the gluten-free demo.

Workstream 2

Gluten-free evidence lab

Independent sources, one product record, provenance on every claim. Each product lands in one of five states and gets a continuous confidence score. The slider sets the operating point.

Pending. The pipeline agent is collecting sources.

Precision and recall at a chosen threshold

Positive class is "gluten-free." A false positive is an unsafe badge on a product with gluten. A false negative is a safe product the filter hides. Products at or above the threshold get the badge.

Pending.

Conflict queue

Products where sources disagree, or where a gluten-free claim sits next to a gluten ingredient. These are where a wrong badge would come from.

Pending.

Label the gold set

Your labels save in this browser only. Keyboard: G gluten-free, N not gluten-free, U unsure, arrow keys to move. Export when done and I will fold them into the pipeline. Once you have labeled 20 or more, the slider measures against your labels instead of the model pre-labels.

Workstream 3, experimental

Label photo reader

A vision model reads the ingredient panel and front of pack, then the read is compared to the listed data. Two models run on every product so the cost and accuracy trade-off is visible. Neither the model nor the crowd-sourced listing is ground truth.
