Instacart Catalog Charter Vision

Hey guys, this is a demo / charter proposal that I created. My hope is that it demonstrates my style as a product leader, as well as what I've learned over the past few weeks through my conversations with all of you.

Every surface reads the same product facts. This vision makes them measurably correct and agent-ready, then makes new attributes cheaper to launch, enabling easier international scale & expansion.

Where to start

1Pain points

Through my discussions with the Catalog team I learned a number of opportunities & pain points. These are seven that I heard vocalized more than once, along with my initial thoughts on solutions to tackle them.

#Pain pointSolution
1New markets — Growth into new markets and onto new platforms will bring data attributes that the current model may not hold: new formats, units, labeling and allergen rules, tax fields and attributes.The right answer is still open, and finding it should be a top priority. Partner with the team leading international expansion to inventory what a new market or platform needs, run a sample feed through the schema and pipelines, and harden what breaks before a launch depends on it.
2Legacy system — The catalog is about ten years old. It is difficult to navigate, and when a value looks wrong it is hard to diagnose why the system produced it.Make the current system explainable first: a living map of how data flows and a decision record on every value. Then stress-test it against the hardest upcoming use cases and rebuild the parts that fail.
3Attribute launch effort — Demand for new attributes keeps growing from search, replacements, health and agents, and each new one still needs its own definition, pipeline design and audit plan. The process is time-consuming and prone to human error.Build on the existing self-serve pipeline platform with a standard attribute spec, reusable templates and quality checks built in, so the tenth attribute costs less than the first.
4Ground truth — Health tags such as gluten-free and allergens need near-perfect precision, and there is no external ground truth to ingest for tags Instacart generates itself.Build on the existing ground truth work. Where golden sets exist, solidify them into release gates. Where they do not, create a small hand-labeled set per attribute so every launch is measured against the same bar.
5Opaque precedence — My understanding is that it’s difficult to explain which values win, and that precedence can differ by attribute and retailer.Record the winning source and the rule that picked it every time a value is written. The same record answers ops escalations and retailer questions.
6Unclear meaning of some attributes — Teams that consume catalog data cannot tell what a field means or how far to trust it.An attribute card for each family, with confidence published next to every value so each consumer sets its own threshold.
7Pipeline decay — Shipped pipelines drift, and some incidents return months later because the fix treated the symptom.A refresh schedule and quality monitor per attribute, plus a recurrence tracker that moves repeat issues up the roadmap.

The road

2Roadmap stages

The stages overlap. Agent work starts in the first stage, because agents are already reading the catalog. Targets are proposals until they are set against measured baselines.

1
Stage one

Trust the facts

Make the attributes that can hurt someone measurably right, and make every value explain itself to people and to agents.

  • Golden sets for allergens and medical diets, created or solidified where they already exist, used as the release gate
  • Every value records which source won and why, at the moment it is written
  • A first agent interface that serves allergen and diet facts with confidence and evidence
Stage targetAllergen values verified on 90% of the items shoppers view, at 99.5% precision, and served to agents with their evidence
2
Stage two

Make attributes cheap

Turn each new attribute from a hand-built project into a repeatable launch.

  • A standard attribute spec and templates on the existing pipeline platform, with quality checks built in
  • Model cascades that send only uncertain items to bigger models and people
  • Refresh schedules and drift monitors on every shipped attribute
  • A schema and pipelines hardened for new markets and new platforms
Stage targetTime and cost to launch a new attribute family cut in half
3
Stage three

Reach every surface

Carry trusted facts into every place a shopper, an agent or a partner meets them.

  • Every attribute family served to agents with its provenance
  • Household restrictions honored in search and in replacements
  • Retailers and brands see and fix their own data problems
Stage targetMost agent answers about dietary fit cite a verified fact

First 90 days

4How I would start

When I join a new firm or team, I like to form and track a 90-day plan. Early priorities are learning, relationship building, and then finding some low-hanging fruit to gain quick wins, earning trust from the team and excitement about forward momentum. Below is a hypothetical beginning, which would be driven by your priorities and objectives for me to tackle, Brittany.

  1. Weeks 1 to 4. Listen, learn, and build relationships! My style of Product Management relies heavily on learning, trusting relationships, and documenting the current & future state. I would map how each consuming team uses catalog data, and measure two high-stakes families against a quick golden set.
  2. Weeks 5 to 8. Create or solidify golden sets and lineage logging for allergens and one medical diet, and expose them through a first agent interface.
  3. Weeks 9 to 12. Write the charter vision with the team, review it up the chain, and set targets against the measured baselines.
Risks and open questions

What could change the plan

Health claims need legal review

Subjective tags change meaning over time. Definitions should be reviewed with legal before an attribute ships.

Model cost at catalog scale

Frontier models are expensive across millions of products. Cascades and caching keep the cost bounded.

Foundation work competes with new attributes

An explicit split of capacity each quarter keeps both moving and makes the trade visible.

5GLP-1 Friendly Catalog

Real products from a Kroger store's public product API, each scored for GLP-1 fit and cross-checked against Open Food Facts and USDA data by UPC. Every attribute shows what each source said and which value won. Ask a question and an AI agent answers from this catalog only, with a citation for every claim.

How are you deciding GLP-1 friendliness?A simple points score from each product's nutrition panel, per serving. +28 g or more protein per 100 calories (+1 for 5 g or more) +115 g or more protein +13 g or more fiber +15 g or less added sugar (−1 above 10 g) +1400 calories or fewer −1More than 5 g saturated fat −1Ultra-processed (NOVA group 4, from Open Food Facts) 4 or more is Friendly, 1 to 3 is Neutral, 0 or less is Not a fit. The signals follow common guidance for people on GLP-1 medications: favor protein and fiber, keep portions modest, and go easy on sugary and high-fat foods. Open any product to see its math. DisclaimerI know this is a pretty primitive solution to a nuanced problem. I mean it only as a demonstration rather than something I'd propose launching to production.
